Our Story

Skolaz was founded with a simple goal: to remove the friction from international student admissions. We built an enterprise platform that connects students, advisors, agencies, and institutions in one seamless workflow.

From the first consultation to enrollment day, Skolaz handles the entire admissions journey — documents, offers, visas, and commissions — all in one place.
